Magnetic rails for cabinetry, doors and movable infrastructure

A rail you cannot feel.

Magnetic suspension and propulsion for drawers, doors, walls and heavy panels, with programmable touch, momentum limits and seismic early warning. Everything on this site is simulated: nothing here has been built or measured in hardware.

Distance to go, on a log scale

"Log-damped" has a precise meaning here: during capture the distance to go is db·e−t/τ, a straight line on a log axis (τ = 0.15 s). A constant-jerk blend leads in; a quintic takes the last 0.2 mm to rest. PHYSICS §16.1.

  1. before tPFloating. Permanent magnets carry the drawer and the coil current settles to zero at equilibrium. Left alone the float would diverge: its open-loop pole is 69.5 rad/s, a 14.4 ms time constant.
  2. tP = 6.67 s after originThe P-wave arrives. At 40 km hypocentral distance it leads the S-wave by 4.76 s. The structure accelerometer picks it; Pd (Wu–Zhao: 0.27 cm for M7) and τc need 1.2 s of signal.
  3. PROTECT at 7.87 sThe drawer glides shut. With the local pick as confirmation, the house choreographer closes open drawers toward the seat at the unattended limit, 0.230 m/s for 48 kg (150 N into a hand behind a 10 N/mm edge). The last 42.3 mm follow the log-damped terminus.
  4. seated at 10.26 sSeated, with time to spare. 0.30 m in 2.19 s plus a 0.2 s z-step onto the captive seat: 1.17 s before the S-wave. The core default for a drawer is simpler: freeze, then stay seated.
  5. tS = 11.43 sThe S-wave arrives. The house sways; the drawer stays seated. After QUIET, nothing re-opens until a manual INSPECT reset and a local restore command.

Momentum is the real limit

Below 147 kg the kinetic-energy cap binds; above it the momentum cap is stricter, which is why a 500 kg wall creeps at 0.0446 m/s unless presence sensing is fitted. HEAVY sits above the momentum line only because it moves while a person holds the button. Closed forms, FAB-SAF-001/002.

Scope, stated honestly.

  • "Zero friction" is a direction, not a claim: the modelled equivalent friction is about 1e-3 to 1e-2, never 0.
  • Everything is simulation. The firmware builds and links for an STM32G474 but has never been flashed; no number on this site is a hardware measurement.
  • No certification or standards compliance is claimed. Limits are checked in simulation against numeric limits from named editions.
  • Contactless only in motion. Drawers rest on a captive seat; walls and heavy panels rest landed. A drawer's open hover is limited by its supercapacitor.
  • The seismic policy is land-first, and the seismic action is not a rated safety function. Isolation and active mass damping are research tier; mass dampers are mainly wind devices.
  • No useful on-site warning closer than about 20 km, and no live early-warning feed: network alerts in the simulator are synthetic inputs.
  • The alert-driven close in the animation is the research-tier house choreographer, moving only toward the seat, at unattended limits, after local confirmation.
  • No whole-building levitation. The villa example reports Coulomb slip events in a sliding-block model and nothing stronger.
  • Frontier items are theoretical and never products.
