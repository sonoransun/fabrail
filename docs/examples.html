Sixteen runnable examples.

Each is a short script. python -m fabrail example list names them; python -m fabrail example run NAME --quick writes a figure and a JSON record to examples/out/NAME/ (full runs are slow-tier).

🔭 frontier Examples 15 and 16 are frontier what-if studies, never product claims; their outputs carry the frontier banner and the orders of magnitude still missing.

How to read a card. The badge is fabrail's own status and never exceeds the weakest requirement the example demonstrates (FAB-EXA-002). Maturity is the technology's readiness in the world, in its own line, and never raises the badge. Every alert in an example is synthetic/injected, not a live EEW feed, and an example on a roadmap class runs on a reduced model and says so.

01

kitchen_push_open

✅ V

Maturity: maglev drawer TRL 3; mechanical push-to-open TRL 9

Push a seated drawer and it lifts off its captive seat, glides open on a log-damped terminus, and later closes back onto the seat the same way. Across 8 to 48 kg the apparent mass stays within ±10 % of the rendered mass.

Haptic lightness does not reduce impact energy: the governor and the monitor use the rated mass. The unseat and slot exit at the full rated 48 kg are under review (capability matrix §1.9).

  • τ 0.15 s
  • d₀ 49.8 mm
  • arrival 0.98 s + 0.2 s seat
  • cap 0.265 m/s at 48 kg
python -m fabrail example run kitchen_push_open --quick

04

ballroom_partition

✅ V

Maturity: hybrid EMS TRL 4–5 at laboratory scale; operable partitions TRL 9 (mechanical)

A 500 kg partition wall feels like 100 kg to the hand (rendered at m/5). It compares the sensed and unsensed timing of the same move, and seals to ±1 mm at the end of travel.

Unsensed, momentum caps it at 0.0446 m/s; presence sensing is what allows faster moves.

  • M_v = m/5
  • τ 0.6 s, arrival 3.56 s
  • seal ±1 mm

archive_heavy_shelving

✅ V

Maturity: HEAVY class research; hybrid EMS TRL 4–5 at laboratory scale

Five-tonne archive shelving moves only while someone holds the button. The momentum governor keeps its speed down even when the payload estimate is sabotaged, because it plans with max(m_rated, 1.05·m̂).

Runs on the Heave1 and Travel1d plants; the six-degree-of-freedom HEAVY case is under review (capability matrix §1.9).

  • hold-to-run 0.025 m/s
  • stop 4.91 mm
  • arrival 5.84 s

drawer_power_loss

✅ V

Maturity: maglev drawer TRL 3; supercapacitor hold-up TRL 9

Cut the power under an open drawer: it runs SS1 Safe Landing on its supercapacitor, rests on its travel rollers, and can still be pushed by hand.

Above 20 kg a failed-down drawer cannot re-lift onto its seat: a manual push to re-seat is required.

wall_pwave_m7

✅ V

Maturity: detector and land-first policy simulated; the ADXL355 structure accelerometer is a catalogue part

An M7 twenty kilometres away. The structure accelerometer picks the P-wave, and a partition wall freezes, lands and locks before the S-wave arrives: land-first.

The seismic action is not a rated safety function (SF-08). Closer than about 20 km there is no useful on-site warning.

synthetic/injected, not a live EEW feed
  • S−P 0.119 s/km
  • decision 1.2 s
  • Pd 0.7118 cm (M7, 20 km)

villa_quake

🧪 Roverall; a badge per panel

Maturity: house choreographer research; each device keeps its class maturity

The flagship: a hillside villa in a synthetic M7.2 at 60 km. Open drawers close toward their seats under unattended limits on log-damped curves, the pantry door lands without locking, the salon wall lands and locks, and every device that could not be secured is reported with its reason.

Contents are reported only as "no Coulomb slip event in the sliding-block model", or as the slip count.

supertall_wind_day

🧪 R

Maturity: TRL 3–4 (1,000 t maglev planar bearing); eddy damping TRL 9

SENTINEL, a 1,000 t hybrid mass damper with an ironless LSM, on a windy day. It activates below 0.3 mg, and the drop in the tower's sway is reported against the tower's own damping.

Mass dampers are mainly wind devices: at design-level shaking they add at most about 0.7 % damping.

  • 44.7 % at μ 1 %, ζs 1 %
  • 30.0 % at ζs 2 %
  • T 7 s, ±1.5 m

vault_close_race

🧪 R

Maturity: TRL 2–3

BASTION's closure race: can a 50 t sliding plug close 3 m and seal before a pressure front arrives? The arrival time is an input; the answer is a close or a fallback.

A close needs the front to arrive at least 5.85 s after detection, a source about 2 km away or more. fabrail publishes no yield-to-distance tables.

  • 3 m in 5.246 s
  • peak 1.144 m/s
  • t_a ≥ 5.85 s

pegs_long_period_securing

🔭 frontier

Maturity: frontier; anchor tier 🧪 network PEGS magnitude (Mw ≥ 8.3)

A far-field Mw 9. A PEGS magnitude advisory that does not saturate, together with a corroborating network alert, secures a heavy stage wagon, a decision that can afford tens of seconds.

lunar_hatch_selene

🔭 frontier

A 100 t hatch in a permanently shadowed lunar crater on passive flux-pinned HTS: what the Moon's gravity and cold do to the open-loop pole, the lift and the radiator budget.
