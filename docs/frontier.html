How far away is it?

Each study asks what would change if a missing material, sensor or scale existed, and says in numbers how far away that is. The numbers are what-if numbers from published theory; none of them describes something that exists or that fabrail builds.

🔭 frontier Frontier tier: theoretical what-if studies, never product claims. Every card names the nearest real, demonstrated capability it extrapolates from (its anchor tier) and states the orders of magnitude still missing. No frontier model drives a controller or a safety function.

    Gravity arrives first: PEGS against the P-wave

    🔭 frontieranchor tier 🧪 PEGS network magnitude, Mw ≥ 8.3FAB-FRN-003FRONTIER §F4

    A rupture moves mass, and the change in gravity travels at the speed of light, so a prompt elasto-gravity signal reaches a site before the P-wave. On an accelerometer it cancels; a gradiometer would see it, at a sensitivity nobody has built. Move the detector noise and watch the race.

    Gradiometer detects–SNR 5 in a 0.1 Hz band
    Dense network alert–station 22 km, 3.3 s latency
    P at the site–6.5 km/s
    Gain over the network– 
    • The need is 1e-15/√Hz (the TOBA Phase-III target); the best demonstrated is 1e-10/√Hz (TOBA Phase-II, 3–8 Hz): five orders short.
    • The fair comparison is a network alert, not the on-site P pick: against a dense P network the lead-time gain at the reference case is about 0 s. It is real only where no station is near the source, offshore or across a border.
    • Even an ideal detector sees only the moment released so far: PEGS can detect early but cannot know the final magnitude early. Its real value is a magnitude that does not saturate, which network PEGS already gives for Mw ≥ 8.3.
    • In fabrail only the network magnitude advisory (research tier) enters fusion, never as a sole trigger (FAB-EXO-810).

    A metabarrier around a building: the mass law

    🔭 frontieranchor tier 🧪 field tests at 30–150 HzFAB-FRN-004FRONTIER §F5

    A seismic metamaterial reflects surface waves in a band near its resonators' frequency. For a fixed relative band gap the resonating mass per area scales as ρ·vs/f, and a barrier about one shear wavelength wide around a site has an area of order λ², so the mass scales as ρλ³: 1.8 Mt at 1 Hz on soil with vs = 100 m/s. Every output states vs.

    Resonating mass against the target frequency
    Resonating mass–ρ·(vs/f)³, ρ 1,800 kg/m³
    In 5 t HEAVY units–each an LSM-trimmed resonator
    Wavelength–λ = vs/f
    Missing orders–3·log₁₀(30 Hz/f)
    • An LSM can retune the band in seconds; it cannot create momentum capacity. The mass is the limit, and the stiffness must be physical springs with the LSM as trim (about 79 kN per 5 t unit at 1 Hz otherwise).
    • Active cancellation instead of reflection would have to absorb ρ·vs·v²·A: about 0.7–9 GW across a 100 × 50 m section at 0.5–1 m/s.
    • No field test exists below 10 Hz, where buildings respond.

    A room-temperature superconductor would not lift more

    🔭 frontieranchor tier 🧪 HTS flux pinningFAB-FRN-001FRONTIER §F2

    No room-temperature superconductor exists. If one did, over a permanent-magnet guideway it could at best exclude the whole field, which caps the pressure at (B₀²/μ₀)·e−2kh: 0.51 MPa at a 10 mm gap for a 154 mm guideway period, about 10× today's HTS and below FeCo EMS at 2.1 MPa. Its value would be passive stability without cryogenics, not force density. Tc, not pressure, is the gap.

    Pressure at 300 K against Tc, for three shielding efficiencies
    Rail-limited ceiling–B₀ 1.2 T, λ 154 mm
    Today's bulk HTS–η ≈ 0.15 of the ideal image
    Tc needed at 300 K–300 × 92/77 (REBCO's reduced temperature)
    • LK-99 was refuted in 2023 and the Dias papers were retracted; LaH₁₀ reaches about 250 K only near 170 GPa.
    • More pinning never lifts the pressure above the ideal image: the ceiling stays rail-limited whatever Tc is.
    Structure-scale magnetic cloak or concentrator: demonstrated only as centimetre laboratory cylinders.

    Making a rail magnetically invisible

    🔭 frontieranchor tier 🧪 cm-scale laboratory cloaksFAB-FRN-007FRONTIER §F8

    An ideal superconducting inner shell inside a ferromagnetic outer shell hides a cylinder from a uniform field when μFM = (R₂² + R₁²)/(R₂² − R₁²). A concentrator packs field inward by R₂/R₁ but can never beat iron: its gain stays at or below Bsat/B₀. The use case is a rail next to MRI or quantum instruments; people need no cloak, because a Halbach array's one-sided field already falls to 0.5 mT within about 1.2 periods.

    SELENE: a 100 t hatch in a permanently shadowed crater

    🔭 frontieranchor tier 🧪 NASA/JPL FLOAT conceptFAB-FRN-006FRONTIER §F7maturity TRL 1–2

    Permanently shadowed regions sit at about 25–40 K, cold enough for REBCO without a cryocooler, but every watt dissipated there needs square metres of ideal radiator. Only passive flux-pinned HTS over permanent magnets, driven only while it moves, fits that budget; moonquakes ring for about an hour, so holding matters more than warning, hence land and lock.

    Demonstrated, so not frontier

    These were relabelled from 🔭 because they exist at some scale; each keeps its own limit.

    Trapped-field bulk magnets

    Multi-tesla bulks exist (17.6 T at 26 K reported). Pulling on steel, the pressure stays at or below 2.1 MPa whatever the trapped field: rail saturation, not the bulk, is the cap.

    ✅ physics FLOAT 🧪 · FAB-FRN-009

    Diamagnetic levitation

    p = |χ|·B²/2μ₀ ≈ 179 Pa at 1 T for pyrolytic graphite, an upper bound: 3.3 orders below an EMS rail at the same field. Consistent with FLOAT's 33 kg/m² under lunar gravity, only 18 kg/m² gross on Earth.

    Quasi-zero-stiffness isolation

    Deployed at instrument scale (zero-power tables, LIGO's HAM-ISI). At building scale the stroke must cover 0.5–2 m against a millimetre gap: 2.7 orders missing. A tilt of 1 mrad reads as 25 mm of false displacement at 0.1 Hz.

    Laboratory-scale magnetic cloak

    A superconducting inner shell with a ferromagnetic outer shell at 77 K in a DC field (2012). Superconductor AC losses rule these out for the travelling field of an LSM.

    documented only

    NV-centre and atom sensing

    Over-specified for gap control: an NV magnetometer at 10 nT/√Hz adds about 2 nm to a drawer's gap estimate. Its frontier role is gradiometry and drift-free tilt references.

    documented only

    DAS along the rail; safe-RL haptics

    A fibre along a long rail is a dense strain array (one feed kind, never a sole trigger). Learned door feel only inside a control-barrier filter that holds the pedestrian caps; the integer monitor stays in place.
